# Research and image sources

Checked October 5, 2026. Approximate planning prices in CAD. Exact October 13–15 operating times are intentionally not promised.

## Attractions

- Niagara City Cruises, Voyage to the Falls: https://www.cityexperiences.com/niagara-ca/city-cruises/voyage-to-the-falls-boat-tour/ (20 minutes on the water, close to Horseshoe Falls, poncho). $47.95 starting base price also shown on the operator's indexed locale page: https://www.cityexperiences.com/da/niagara-ca/city-cruises/voyage-to-the-falls-boat-tour/ . Ontario HST at 13% yields $54.18, rounded to ~$54. Checkout may change by date or fees.
- Great Canadian Midway: https://www.cliftonhill.com/attractions/great-canadian-midway . 300+ games; free entry, paid credits. Current credit packages start at $10 + HST; new in-person cards cost $2 + HST. Hours are subject to change, so the site does not promise a specific closing time.
- Niagara Glen: https://www.niagaraparks.com/visit/attractions/niagara-glen . Rugged trails, stairs, Niagara River and old-growth forest. Current notices close part of Terrace Trail and Whirlpool Trail. Hiking daylight only. Allowing two hours is our itinerary choice, not an official fixed duration.
- Fall scenery: https://www.niagaraparks.com/fall-in-love-with-nature-at-niagara-parks . Foliage varies with weather.
- Whirlpool Aero Car: https://www.niagaraparks.com/visit/attractions/whirlpool-aero-car . Adult admission $25; approximate visit 45 minutes including variable waiting; seasonal April–November; free parking. The website uses the current English page rather than older translated price listings.
- Ghost Walks: https://ghostwalks.com/niagara-on-the-lake . Adult $17 + HST and 1.5% fee, 90-minute outdoor walk, nightly September through Halloween. Check October 14's specific departure time.
- White Water Walk: https://www.niagaraparks.com/visit/attractions/white-water-walk . A paid, seasonal flexible-day option, not included in the core subtotal.
- COWS: https://cows.ca/pages/locations . Official Niagara-on-the-Lake location listing.

## Food

- Country Fresh Donuts & More, soup and donuts: https://www.chch.com/chch-news/famous-won-ton-soup-niagara-falls-donut-shop-reopens/ . Historical local reporting, supported by current listing/reviews: https://directoryniagara.ca/listing/country-fresh-donuts-niagara-falls . Recheck hours and payment options.
- Napoli Ristorante: https://www.napoliristorante.ca/location and https://www.napoliristorante.ca/copy-of-about-hours . Ferry/Stanley, free parking, Tuesday–Thursday opening currently 4:30 PM. No exact restaurant times are committed in the itinerary.
- Tide & Vine Oyster House: https://tideandvine.com/oyster-house/ . Seafood and oysters, 3491 Portage Road, reservation option for groups of 1–8.
